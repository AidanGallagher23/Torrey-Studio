How I work

A few things that matter to me.

01

Honesty first

I won't take a project I can't do well, and I won't pad a scope to make an invoice look bigger. If something's outside my abilities right now, I'll say so.

02

Free sample, always

Every project starts with a sample of the actual work — not a pitch, not a mood board. You'll see what you're buying before any money changes hands.

03

One point of contact

You'll work directly with me throughout. That means faster feedback loops, fewer miscommunications, and a final product that reflects one consistent voice.

04

Clear pricing

Flat-fee quotes based on what's actually needed. No vague hourly estimates, no surprise add-ons. You'll know the number before anything starts.
