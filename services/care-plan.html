What this doesn't cover.

Straight talk

The care plan covers small, defined changes — not new page builds, design overhauls, or significant feature additions. Those are quoted separately as one-off projects.

It also doesn't cover third-party platform issues (if Netlify is down, that's not a me problem), domain registration or renewal, or anything that would realistically take more than a couple of hours to implement.

If something comes in that's outside the scope of maintenance, I'll tell you upfront and give you a separate quote for it instead of billing you for extra hours without warning.
